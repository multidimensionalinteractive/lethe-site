THE ENCLOSURE HOLDS

You Are Still Inside It is a long-form body of work on the Eastern Front, with Stalingrad at its gravitational center.

It constructs a historical interior where the figures are presences, not symbols.

Unknown men whose unresolved narratives rise from a rupture that never fully closed.

The meta-anomaly remains: the subject who witnessed himself never witnessed the printed image.

One of many unresolved ends on the Eastern Front, which I relate to light and manifest through my eyes.

09

The process begins in a Jungian, pre-verbal register — a bodily recognition, permission granted from within the image itself.

10

Lethe moves through it: both forgetting and the refusal to let remain forgotten.

This is clarification and realignment.
