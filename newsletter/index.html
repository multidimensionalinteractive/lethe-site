FIELD CORRESPONDENCE

A quiet weekly letter from the archive — not announcements, but notes from inside the work.

This is a correspondence list for people who want to stay close to the Lethe project as it unfolds — the research, the studio, and the material that does not always appear on the site immediately.

About once a week, a short letter goes out with what is active in the archive: passages from ongoing field research, poems and prose as they are finished, paintings and studies in progress, links to new Field Observations, and occasional notes on sources, maps, and objects under examination.

It is written in the same spirit as the site itself — restrained, archival, oriented toward the Eastern Front and the human interior of catastrophic history. It is not a marketing list. There are no discounts, no funnels, no urgency. If nothing substantial is ready, the week may pass quietly.
